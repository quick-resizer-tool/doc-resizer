How to Resize Images for Job & Exam Applications

Most online examination and employment registration portals enforce rigid file limits—typically requiring passport photos to stay between 20 KB and 50 KB, and digital signatures between 10 KB and 20 KB. Submitting files outside these boundaries often leads to upload rejections or form errors.

Complete Privacy & Security

Unlike standard online conversion portals that upload files to cloud servers, this tool executes 100% of processing locally inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas. Your sensitive identity documents, certificates, and signatures never travel over the network or get saved to a remote database.
