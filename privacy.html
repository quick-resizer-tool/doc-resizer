Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to DocResize. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when you use our website and browser-based utility tools.

1. Document & Image Privacy (Client-Side Only)

We believe in absolute data privacy. All file resizing, conversions, and image compression happen entirely within your device's web browser using HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript:

  • Your photos, signatures, and personal documents are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud.
  • We do not store, copy, monitor, or transmit your files over the network.
  • Once you close the browser tab, any temporary memory used for image processing is immediately discarded.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require account registration, passwords, credit card numbers, phone numbers, or mailing addresses to use our free document resizing tools.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies (Google AdSense)

We may partner with third-party advertising networks, such as Google AdSense, to display advertisements to support our free service. Please note:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Log Files & Analytics

Like standard web platforms, our hosting provider may automatically log non-identifiable technical data such as browser type, operating system, referring pages, and visit timestamps to maintain uptime and ensure server security.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at our official contact page.
